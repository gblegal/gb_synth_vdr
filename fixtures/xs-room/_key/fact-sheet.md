## Deal summary

Halstead Fasteners Limited, a precision-fastener manufacturer trading as
HalstedConnect and selling under the FastenTrack product range from its
Wexbury Industrial Estate site, is being sold by its parent, Bramcote
Industries Holdings Limited, to a financial sponsor by way of a sale of the
whole issued share capital. The data room supports legal due diligence ahead
of signing.
